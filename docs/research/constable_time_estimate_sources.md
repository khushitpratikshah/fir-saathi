# Constable time estimate sources

## Independent report-writing study

Ian T. Adams, Matt Barter, Kyle McLean, Hunter Boehme, and Irick A. Geary, *No Man’s Hand: Artificial Intelligence Does Not Improve Police Report Writing Speed*, CrimRxiv, published 2024-09-11, DOI 10.21428/cb6ab371.4a6d42e9: https://www.crimrxiv.com/pub/nxbmzp2j/release/1

The pre-registered randomized controlled trial involved 85 police officers and 755 reports. The paper reports a historical mean report-writing time of 54.63 minutes (SD 47.18) and describes a 10-minute reduction as a conservative effect size for its power analysis. Its actual result was null: AI assistance did not significantly affect report-writing duration, including a full-year difference-in-differences analysis over 6,084 reports. It concludes that no time savings in report-writing should be assumed from AI-assisted report writing alone.

README use: present a 5–10 minute per eligible intake planning estimate only, not as a measured FIR Saathi result. The calculation is a transparent scenario assumption of 10–20% of the 54.63-minute baseline (5.46–10.93 minutes, rounded to 5–10), because FIR Saathi addresses intake structuring and clarification rather than replacing an officer’s full report. The study’s null result is a reason to label the estimate as unvalidated and to require a local pilot.

## U.S. DOJ COPS Office context

U.S. Department of Justice COPS Office, *Using AI to Write Police Reports*, January 2025: https://cops.usdoj.gov/html/dispatch/01-2025/ai_reports.html

The article describes agencies using audio transcription and AI-generated first drafts, but says officers still review the draft, fill in missing details, manually edit, and sign off. It also reports concerns around non-English speakers and notes that the systems are used cautiously in some agencies. The article contains agency/user testimonials about time savings, not an independent causal estimate.

README use: cite this source for the need for human review and the distinction between an AI-assisted draft and a completed official report; do not use its testimonials as a numerical FIR Saathi impact claim.
